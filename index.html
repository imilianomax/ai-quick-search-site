A simple Chrome utility

One search.
Multiple AIs.

Highlight text on a webpage or type a prompt in the toolbar, then open it in one AI assistant or up to three separate tabs.

Version 3.0 information. Check the store listing for the currently available version.

View on Chrome Web StoreRead our privacy policy

Your text. Your choice of assistant.

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek or Google AI Mode. Answers stay on each service’s own website.

Search selected text

Select text, right-click and choose an assistant. Or start a new question from the extension’s toolbar popup.

Open up to three

Send the same prompt to two or three selected destinations in separate tabs. The extension does not combine their answers.

Keep control

Choose your assistants and their order. Gemini and DeepSeek use optional site access for prompt prefilling.

No additional extension account or API key is required. Provider login, plans, availability and manual submission may apply.
